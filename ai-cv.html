AI Engineering Practices

🌙

Privacy-First AI

On-device ML at the edge for sensitive data. Core ML models on iOS, ML Kit on Android — no data leaves the device. Zero-knowledge processing for PII.

⚡

Low-Latency Inference

Model quantization, pruning, and hardware-accelerated inference. Sub-100ms response targets for real-time AI features during user interaction.

RAG with confidence thresholds, citation back-ends, and ground-truth verification layers. Fallback mechanisms when AI uncertainty exceeds safe operating bounds.

Agentic Architectures

Multi-agent orchestration for complex tasks: research agents, content agents, and validation agents working in sequence or parallel for robust AI workflows.
